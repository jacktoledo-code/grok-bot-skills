# Cross-border household taxes

A family that lives in one country and still has accounts, income, or tax history in another can use an AI assistant to keep the year in one place. The usual shape is a US household with Canadian accounts. The same steps work for any two-country setup.

## What it does

The assistant helps with the paperwork around the return:

- Organize cloud-drive folders by country and year
- Keep a document checklist (have / requested / N/A)
- Maintain a foreign-account table for FBAR and Form 8938
- Manage email with two accountants by drafting replies the human sends
- Review the preparer's draft before filing

The assistant organizes and checks. Licensed preparers prepare and file.

## Who it's for

Households filing in more than one country who want one place for documents, accountant questions, and a last look at the draft return.

## What to connect

- Email, so the assistant can find accountant threads and draft replies
- A cloud drive, for folders, PDFs, and notes
- Read-only bank links, for balances and transactions
- Accountant and tax portals, with you signing in and entering any codes

## How to use it

Add `SKILL.md` in this folder as a skill in Grok Bot, or point any agent that reads `SKILL.md` at this file. The assistant follows the skill. You approve drafts and sign in yourself.

## A real run

It organized a full year across two countries. The review caught a foreign account missing from the FBAR before filing.

## Disclaimer

This is not tax advice. It organizes documents and checks a draft. Licensed preparers file the returns.
